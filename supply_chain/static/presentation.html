Section 01 // Problem Statement PRD §2 • Challenges in Global Supply Chains

Why Traditional Supply Chains Fail

Opacity & Counterfeits

Over $500B lost annually to counterfeit products due to untraceable multi-tier handoffs and fraudulent origin documentation.

Centralized Alterations

Traditional relational ERPs permit privileged DBA users to silently alter audit logs, change ownership dates, or rewrite rejection rates.

IoT Hardware Fallibility

RFID tags, GPS beacons, and ESP32 sensors fail in real-world transit: battery drainage, physical tampering, signal jamming, and high unit cost.

The Solution: Replace fallible hardware trust with mathematical trust—X.509 cryptographic identities, Raft consensus, Merkle proofs, and AI risk analytics.

Section 08 // Decentralized Storage & Merkle Trees PRD §6 (FR-07 & FR-08)

IPFS Storage & O(log N) Merkle Proofs

IPFS Content Addressing (CIDv1)

Off-chain document storage for phytosanitary certificates, invoices, and lab tests. Only the cryptographic hash and Content Identifier travel on-chain.

Hash: SHA-256 (32 Bytes)
CID: bafybeic... (Base32 CIDv1)
Tamper Status: Zero-Byte Bitflip Detection

Binary Merkle Tree Verification

Enables batch verification of thousands of supply chain documents with a single 32-byte on-chain Merkle root.

Complexity: O(log N) Verification
Proof: Cryptographic audit sibling path
Integrity: Mathematically Incorruptible
Efficiency Gain: Storing 1,000 certificates directly costs megabytes on-chain; Merkle trees reduce ledger footprint to a single 32-byte root.
Zero Ledger Bloat Scalable to Millions of Documents
